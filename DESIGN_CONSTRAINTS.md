# APMS design constraints

Apply to every future design revision. Structure first. Animation only after the layout is settled.

Avoid harsh gradients, Lucide icons, pure white backgrounds, rainbow colors, drop shadows, emojis, liquid glass, em dashes, Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, colored left stripes, fake testimonials, bento grids, terminal windows, contrast slogans, checkmark bullets, three-tier pricing layouts, soft corner radii, purple and black palettes, radial orbs, dot grids, sparkle icons, animated arrows, hover animations, neon colors, and basic pastel palettes.

Include useful loading skeletons. Do not present an explanatory illustration as a real product demo. Terms and privacy information must reflect actual institutional practices and must be present before public launch.

Current structural direction: warm paper backgrounds, ink typography, restrained burnt orange, flat square controls, task-first lists and clear horizontal separators. Avoid decorative dashboard captions. Keep essential actions above statistics. Preserve authentic empty states and actual data.
